About the club

We make the ordinary week
a little stranger.

SOMA started five years ago in a dorm common room with three easels and a bag of charcoal someone's roommate never asked for back. It's grown into a standing collective of painters, printmakers, photographers, sculptors, and people who just like being around work in progress.

We meet weekly to draw, critique, and occasionally argue about whether a piece is finished. Membership is open to anyone at the university, regardless of major or experience — some of our strongest exhibitors picked up a brush for the first time at one of our workshops.

What we're really building is a room where making something half-good in public doesn't feel like a risk. The gallery on this site is pulled entirely from member submissions, unedited and unranked.

Get involved

Make something
with us.

The club runs entirely on people who show up. We need hands for gallery installs, eyes for jurying open submissions, and voices for running weekly life-drawing sessions — no experience required for most of it.

Volunteering here means real responsibility fast: past volunteers have hung full shows, edited the annual print zine, and taught their own workshops within a semester.
